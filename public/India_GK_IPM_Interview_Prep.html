📖 About Union Territories
What is a Union Territory (UT)? A UT is directly governed by the Central Government, unlike states which have their own elected governments. Some UTs (Delhi, Puducherry, J&K) have partial statehood with elected assemblies. The Centre can override their decisions. They are created for special administrative, strategic, or constitutional reasons.
UTs WITH Legislature
  • 🏛 Delhi (NCT) — Has Legislative Assembly; LG has special powers under Art. 239AA
  • 🏛 Puducherry — Has Legislative Assembly; operates like a mini-state
  • 🏛 Jammu & Kashmir — Has Legislative Assembly (since 2020 as UT); was a full state until Oct 31, 2019
UTs WITHOUT Legislature
  • 📍 Ladakh — Separated from J&K in 2019; no legislature
  • 📍 Andaman & Nicobar Islands
  • 📍 Lakshadweep
  • 📍 Chandigarh
  • 📍 Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu (merged 2020)
